📜 All 40 Scroll Magic Phrases 📜

About These Phrases

Each game randomly assigns these magical phrases to the 7 scroll types. The same phrase means different things in different games!

Classic References

  • XYZZY, PLUGH - Colossal Cave Adventure
  • ZELGO MER, JUYED AWK - NetHack
  • ELAM EBOW - "BOW MALE" scrambled
  • ELBIB YLOH - "HOLY BIBLE" reversed
  • FROBOZZ - Zork
  • HACKEM MUCHE - NetHack variant
  • LOREM IPSUM - Classic placeholder

In Your Game

When you start a new game, 7 of these 40 phrases are randomly chosen for the 7 scroll types:

  1. Psionic Scroll - Mental attack on visible monsters
  2. Teleport Scroll - Random teleportation
  3. Mapping Scroll - Reveal dungeon layout
  4. Fireball Scroll - Area damage
  5. Regeneration Scroll - Heal over time
  6. Enchantment Scroll - Enhance equipment
  7. Uncurse Scroll - Remove curse

Example: In one game, "XYZZY" might teleport you. In the next game, "XYZZY" might cast fireball!
